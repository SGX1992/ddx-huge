The main event

A live creative battle.
Real brief. Real clock.
Real AI.

MAKE.EXE is Huge's live creative competition: multidisciplinary teams use AI to transform a mystery brief into bold storytelling concepts in real time, in front of the clients who wrote it and a jury that scores it on the spot.

The first edition ran in Los Angeles in October 2025 — Huge creatives and design students answering surprise briefs from clients, round after round, with the tools of the moment. Huge calls it the start of the esports era for creative design. New York is the second edition, and the reason you're reading this.

Your evening

One hour before the doors.
A room of thirty.

The VIP hour is small on purpose: the people who run Huge, the creatives about to compete, and a handful of guests DDX has chosen. Then everyone walks downstairs for the battle.

  1. 4:30 PM

    VIP hour on the mezzanine

    Curated drinks, passed hors d'oeuvres, and a tour of the space before it fills. A brief welcome from Josh Campo, CEO of Huge.

  2. ~5:00 PM

    The creatives, and one question

    Huge's Chief Creative Officer introduces the team about to go on the clock — professionals and students alike — and puts one question to the room: what will shape brand and customer experience in the next twelve months? Unfiltered conversation follows, with Josh Campo and Eric Vienna, Group Creative Director, on how MAKE.EXE started and what an AI-native studio actually looks like.

  3. 5:30 PM

    Doors open downstairs

    The main MAKE.EXE audience arrives. You keep your drink and your head start.

  4. 6:00 PM

    The clock starts

    The mystery brief is revealed and the competition begins, with live judging. Stay as long as you like.
